COMMON QUESTIONS

Broward fix and flip financing questions

How fast can I get fix and flip financing in Broward County? ▾
Underlytix scores your deal's fundability in about 60 seconds and, if it qualifies, routes it to the lender in our network whose criteria it meets. Actual funding timelines still depend on that lender's underwriting, appraisal, and closing process — Underlytix is the pre-application step, not the loan itself.
What do fix and flip lenders in Broward County look for? ▾
Most hard money and bridge lenders active in Broward County (Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pompano Beach, Coral Springs, and nearby) underwrite primarily on after-repair value (ARV), loan-to-cost, rehab budget and draw schedule, and borrower experience — more than personal income. Underlytix models your deal against these criteria before you approach a lender.
Does Underlytix lend money directly for fix and flip deals in Broward? ▾
No. Underlytix is not a lender. It scores whether your deal is fundable and matches fundable deals to the private lender in its network whose criteria the deal meets. All financing decisions, terms, and closings are handled by that lender through their own underwriting.
Can I get fix and flip financing in Broward with no experience? ▾
It's harder but not automatically disqualifying — some lenders in the Underlytix network work with first-time flippers at a lower loan-to-cost or with a general contractor requirement. Underlytix flags exactly what would need to change (more cash to close, a smaller rehab scope, a GC on file) rather than giving a flat no.
