Who Each Is Built For

Different segment of the financing market

1

Deal scale Underlytix: individual deals

Lev's underwriting agent is built around commercial sponsors and brokers uploading full deal packages, rent rolls, and financial statements for large transactions. Underlytix is built for the individual investor or realtor client deal, DSCR rentals, fix-and-flip, bridge, cash-out refinance, new construction, and smaller commercial financing.

2

Who initiates the process

Lev's workflow assumes a broker or sponsor in the loop, uploading documents for underwriting. Underlytix lets the investor or realtor run their own deal and get a score directly, no broker required to start.

3

Time to a result

Lev's underwriting agent works from rent rolls, financial statements, and comps to produce review-ready underwriting, a multi-day process. Underlytix produces a Capital Readiness Score, DSCR, LTV, cash-on-cash, in about 60 seconds.

4

Lender matching

Lev matches underwritten deals against its lender database by program, geography, and deal size. Underlytix matches the scored deal to lenders by deal type, LTV range, and loan size, and includes a dedicated realtor portal for client buyer prequalification that Lev doesn't offer.
