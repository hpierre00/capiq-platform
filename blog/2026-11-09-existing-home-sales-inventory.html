Mortgage Rates Ease to 7.18% as Inventory Builds

Underlytix Desk4 min read

The 30-year fixed mortgage rate averaged 7.18% in the week ended November 5, 2026, according to Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey. That is down from 7.24% the prior week and from a peak of 7.40% in the surveys of October 8 and October 15, but it remains well above the 6.22% recorded a year earlier. For investors, the rate has eased for three consecutive weeks without returning to anything close to last year's financing costs.

The latest National Association of Realtors report, released October 13 and covering September, shows a market that is moving slowly rather than freezing. Existing-home sales ran at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 3.99 million, up 1.5% from August but down 2.2% from September 2025. The median existing-home price was $417,500, up 1.3% year over year, so price growth has nearly stalled while transaction volume remains subdued.

Sources: Freddie Mac PMMS via FRED (weeks of October 8 to November 5, 2026); NAR existing-home sales release, October 13, 2026.

Supply is the more useful signal. Unsold inventory reached 1.60 million homes, up 4.6% from a year earlier, which equals 4.8 months of supply versus 4.5 months a year ago. Median time on market was 33 days, unchanged from September 2025. Cash purchases made up 27% of sales, down from 30% a year earlier, while individual investors and second-home buyers accounted for 20% of transactions, up from 15% in both August and September 2025. Buyers with financing are carrying a larger share of the market, and investor participation is rising even as rates stay elevated.

Taken together, the data points to modest negotiating room rather than a buyer's market. Supply is rising from a year ago, yet days on market have not lengthened. Investors underwriting acquisitions this week should assume financing costs near 7% for conventional loans, treat price discounts as deal specific, and stress test cash flow against the possibility that rates stay in this range.

What this means for deals under review this month: At a 30-year rate above 7%, debt service coverage and cash-to-close can swing a deal from viable to marginal. Until then, the verified figures support disciplined underwriting: conservative rate assumptions, realistic closing costs, and a clear view of liquidity before committing to a purchase.
