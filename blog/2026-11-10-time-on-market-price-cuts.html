Homes Take 50 Days to Sell at 98.3% of List as Price Cuts Reach 21.5% of Listings

Underlytix Desk4 min read

The U.S. housing market is moving slowly, but it is not repricing sharply. In the four weeks ending October 25, 2026, Redfin reported a median of 50 days on market, an average sale-to-list price ratio of 98.3%, and 23.2% of homes selling above list price. Both the sale-to-list ratio and the above-list share were unchanged from a year earlier. Redfin also reported that 21.5% of listings had a price drop, and that seasonally adjusted pending sales of 276,352 were down 15.9% year over year, the lowest level in the firm's records.

Realtor.com's October 2026 Housing Report, published November 4, points the same direction. The national median days on market was 63, up two days from September and one day from October 2025. A preliminary 20.5% of active listings carried a price reduction, up 0.2 percentage points from a year earlier. Active listings rose 7.4% year over year to 1,183,500, while new listings fell 7.5% to 357,000. Realtor.com also reported that the median list price was $415,000, down 2.2% from a year earlier, and that contract signings were down 17%.

Sources: Redfin (four weeks ending October 25, 2026); Realtor.com October 2026 Housing Report (published November 4, 2026). Methodologies differ.

The two sources should not be compared level to level. Redfin and Realtor.com define and sample days on market differently, which is why one reports 50 days and the other 63. The direction is what matters, and it is consistent: slightly longer marketing periods, steady price cuts, and thinner demand. Realtor.com's year-over-year change in days on market was one day in the Northeast, three in the Midwest, one in the South and zero in the West, so the slowdown is modest rather than uniform.

The pricing signal is mixed. A sale-to-list ratio of 98.3% means the typical sale closes within roughly two percent of asking, and nearly one in four homes still sells above list. Sellers are not capitulating. At the same time, Redfin's months of supply rose to 4.5 from 4, and its median monthly mortgage payment was $2,786, up 10.7% from a year earlier at a 7.32% weekly average rate as of October 25. Demand is constrained by financing cost more than by price.

What this means for deals under review this month: Slower markets reward preparation. When days on market lengthen and price cuts persist, negotiating room appears, but it only helps a buyer who can move quickly and finance with certainty.
